# Product Manual (Demo)

## Product Overview
The Acme Platform is a subscription-based analytics product with three tiers:
- Basic Plan (USD 9.99/month): dashboards and CSV export.
- Pro Plan (USD 29.99/month): dashboards, CSV/PDF export, and email alerts.
- Enterprise Plan (USD 99.99/month): everything in Pro plus SSO, audit logs, and SLA.

## Onboarding
- New customers receive a welcome email with a verification link that expires in 48 hours.
- After email verification, users can create up to 3 workspaces on the Basic Plan, 10 on Pro, and unlimited on Enterprise.
- The onboarding checklist appears on the home dashboard until at least one report is created.

## Data Retention
- Basic Plan: raw event data retained for 30 days.
- Pro Plan: raw event data retained for 180 days.
- Enterprise Plan: raw event data retained for 365 days, with optional long-term archival.
- Deleted workspaces are recoverable for 14 days before permanent purge.

## Support
- Basic Plan: community forum and email support with 3 business day SLA.
- Pro Plan: email support with 1 business day SLA.
- Enterprise Plan: 24x7 email and phone support with 4 hour SLA for Sev-1 incidents.
- Support hours for phone are 08:00 to 20:00 UTC.

## Integrations
- Slack, Microsoft Teams, and PagerDuty are supported on Pro and Enterprise plans.
- Custom webhooks are available on all plans.
- SSO via SAML and OIDC is Enterprise-only.
