# Company Policy Manual (Demo)

## Leave Policy
- All full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per year.
- Sick leave is capped at 10 days per year and requires a doctor's note beyond 3 consecutive days.
- Parental leave is 16 weeks paid for primary caregivers and 4 weeks paid for secondary caregivers.
- Unused annual leave up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar year.

## Remote Work Policy
- Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
- Fully remote arrangements are available only for approved roles listed in the internal HR portal.
- Employees working remotely must be reachable during their local business hours (9am-5pm local time).

## Expense Policy
- Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to USD 75 per day.
- Airfare must be booked in economy class for flights under 6 hours.
- Hotel expenses are reimbursed up to USD 250 per night in Tier 1 cities and USD 150 per night elsewhere.
- All receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.

## Security Policy
- Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled at all times.
- All employees must enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on corporate accounts.
- Sharing passwords with anyone, including IT, is strictly prohibited.
- Report suspected phishing to security@company.example within 24 hours.
